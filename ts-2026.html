The C&W Berry Trade Show returns on 8th, 9th & 10th September at our Main Sales Site on Golden Hill Lane. With more than 135 leading brands represented, our flagship event has become one of the North West's premier supplier showcases for the construction industry.

With unrivalled supplier attendance and thousands of visitors expected, the 2026 Trade Show promises to be our biggest and best event yet.
